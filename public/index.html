Safe backup & restore

Exports do not include your optional TMDB credential. Keep downloaded backups private: they contain your viewing history and notes.

To restore a SQLite backup: stop the app, keep a copy of your existing data folder, replace library.db with the backup, remove only library.db-wal and library.db-shm from that folder if present, then launch Movies of Interest from the Start menu.

Updates keep your library. Each time a new version first starts, it saves a safety copy in the backups subfolder of the data folder (the five most recent are kept); restore one the same way.

Metadata & streaming

Keyless mode uses Wikidata / Wikipedia for movies and TVmaze for TV. Some facts and images may be missing. Service search links do not verify availability, and some services may require searching again after sign-in.

A TMDB read access token enables richer new-title lookups, trailers and regional watch guides. Existing keyless entries keep their source; add a TMDB match to enrich one. Refresh TMDB entries after changing country.

Application controls

The installed app starts at Windows sign-in and remains available while you are signed in. It cannot serve pages when your PC is off or asleep. Closing this browser tab does not stop the app.

Stop server: disconnects this site now; your data stays intact. Restart from the Start menu, or at your next sign-in. This action is reversible.

Uninstall: opens the Windows uninstaller and removes the app and its sign-in startup. The uninstaller separately asks whether to permanently delete the local database. Deleted data cannot be recovered without a backup.
